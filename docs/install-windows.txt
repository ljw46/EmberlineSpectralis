EMBERLINE SPECTRALIS - Windows install
======================================

This folder contains two things:

  Emberline Spectralis.vst3   the plugin, for your DAW (Reaper, Ableton, FL Studio...)
  Emberline Spectralis.exe    the Standalone app: double-click it to play without a DAW

PLUGIN (VST3)
  1. Copy the whole "Emberline Spectralis.vst3" item into:
         C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
     (Windows may ask for permission - click Continue.)
  2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. It appears as "Emberline Spectralis", an instrument.

STANDALONE
  Double-click "Emberline Spectralis.exe". Pick your audio device when asked.

"Windows protected your PC" warning?
  The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows is cautious. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Manual: click the ? button next to the preset bar inside the plugin.
